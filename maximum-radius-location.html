The maximum radius for a work location in Tamam is typically configurable by administrators, with practical limits based on GPS accuracy and business requirements.

Standard Radius Settings

  • Minimum: 50 meters (for precise office locations)
  • Typical: 100-200 meters (standard office buildings)
  • Maximum: 500+ meters (for large campuses or buildings)

Factors Affecting Radius

When setting a location radius, consider:

  • Building size: Larger buildings need larger radii
  • GPS accuracy: Urban areas have better GPS accuracy (±5-10 meters)
  • Parking areas: Must be within the radius for field staff
  • Security: Smaller radii reduce unauthorized check-ins from nearby areas

Configuring Location Radius

Administrators can:

  • Set custom radius for each location based on needs
  • Use map visualization to preview the coverage area
  • Adjust radius after analyzing check-in patterns
  • Set different radii for different locations

Testing Your Radius

After setting up locations:

  • Test check-ins from various points within the radius
  • Verify that valid check-in points are accepted
  • Ensure that points outside the radius are rejected

Tip: Start with a 150-meter radius and adjust based on employee feedback and check-in patterns.
