Appearance

Customize the app appearance, logo, and branding for your organization.

Appearance settings help your organization keep a consistent visual identity in Tamam.

Step 1: Access appearance settings

Open the Appearance or Branding settings in the admin console.

Step 2: Upload your logo

Upload your organization logo and choose how it displays in the app and reports.

Step 3: Adjust colors

Select accent colors or theme preferences if available.

Step 4: Preview changes

Preview the new appearance on desktop and mobile screens.

Step 5: Save and verify

Save the appearance settings and confirm the branding updates are visible throughout the app.

Tip: Use the same logo and colors across Tamam to make the experience familiar for employees.
