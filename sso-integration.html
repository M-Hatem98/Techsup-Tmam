Contact Support

Yes, Tamam supports Single Sign-On (SSO) integration, enabling seamless authentication with your organization's identity management systems.

Supported SSO Protocols

  • SAML 2.0 (Security Assertion Markup Language)
  • OAuth 2.0
  • OpenID Connect (OIDC)
  • LDAP/Active Directory

Popular SSO Providers

Tamam integrates with:

  • Microsoft Azure Active Directory
  • Okta
  • Google Workspace
  • OneLogin
  • ForgeRock
  • Ping Identity
  • Custom LDAP servers

SSO Benefits

  • Single login for multiple applications
  • Improved user experience
  • Reduced password management burden
  • Better security through centralized authentication
  • Automatic user provisioning and deprovisioning
  • Audit trail through central identity provider

Setting Up SSO

  1. Prepare your SSO provider details and credentials
  2. Contact Tamam support to configure SSO
  3. Provide federation metadata or configuration details
  4. Test SSO authentication in staging
  5. Deploy to production

User Experience

With SSO enabled:

  • Users click "Login with [Provider Name]"
  • They authenticate via your organization's identity system
  • They are automatically logged into Tamam
  • No separate Tamam credentials needed

Tip: Contact our implementation team to discuss SSO options for your organization.
