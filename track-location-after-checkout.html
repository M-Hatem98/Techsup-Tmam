No, Tamam does not track employee location after they check out. Location tracking is limited to official work hours and check-in/checkout times.

Privacy and Tracking Policy

Tamam respects employee privacy by:

  • Only tracking location during authorized work periods
  • Stopping location tracking after checkout
  • Not monitoring employee movements outside work hours
  • Complying with privacy regulations and laws
  • Storing minimal location history

What Gets Tracked

  • Check-in Location: Recorded at the time of check-in
  • Check-out Location: Recorded at the time of checkout
  • Work Hours: Time spent at the authorized location
  • Attendance Status: Present, absent, or late

After Checkout

Once an employee checks out:

  • Location tracking stops immediately
  • GPS location is no longer recorded
  • No background tracking occurs
  • The employee's personal time is their own

Data Retention

Location data is:

  • Retained only for reporting purposes
  • Accessible only to authorized administrators
  • Subject to organization's data retention policy
  • Used only for attendance management

Tip: Always check out when leaving your work location to stop GPS tracking and ensure data accuracy.
