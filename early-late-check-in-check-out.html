Early/Late Check In and Check Out

Manage and track early and late attendance exceptions in Tamam.

The Early/Late Check In and Check Out feature helps administrators identify and manage attendance exceptions outside scheduled working hours.

Step 1: Review Early/Late Records

Navigate to the attendance section and open the early/late check-in and check-out records. These show employees deviating from shift times.

Step 2: Analyze Early Check-Ins

Review employees who checked in earlier than scheduled. Determine if this is due to shift changes, flexibility, or system errors.

Step 3: Analyze Late Check-Ins

Check which employees are consistently arriving late. Look for patterns that suggest productivity or behavioral issues needing attention.

Step 4: Review Early/Late Check-Outs

Examine check-out times as well. Early departures might indicate approved leave or violations of policy.

Step 5: Take Corrective Action

Contact managers about significant exceptions and work with HR to address chronic patterns. Use the data to inform attendance policies.

Tip: Set grace periods and thresholds in your attendance policies to distinguish between acceptable timing variations and actual violations.
