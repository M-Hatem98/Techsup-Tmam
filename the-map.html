The Map feature provides a visual representation of employee locations during check-in, helping verify that employees are at their assigned work sites.

Step 1: Open the Map View

Navigate to the Map section from the main dashboard. This displays a geographic view of employee locations.

Step 2: View Employee Locations

Check employee positions on the map during check-in times. The system shows which employees are within the allowed location radius.

Step 3: Identify Location Anomalies

Look for employees checking in from unexpected locations. This helps detect unauthorized remote work or location fraud.

Step 4: Review Location History

Click on employee markers to view their location check-in history. Compare multiple days to spot patterns or inconsistencies.

Step 5: Take Action on Issues

If location violations occur, review the employee record and contact the manager. Use the data to adjust location policies if needed.

Tip: Use the map feature alongside location-based attendance rules to ensure compliance with workplace requirements.
