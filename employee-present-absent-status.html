Employee present/Absent Status

Monitor who is currently present or absent using live attendance status tracking.

Use the status view to see live attendance and spot absent employees quickly.

Step 1: Access attendance status

From the dashboard, open the attendance overview and choose the present/absent status report.

Step 2: Apply filters

Select date, location, department, or team filters to focus the status view.

Step 3: Review present employees

Check the list of employees marked present and confirm their check-in times.

Step 4: Review absent employees

Look at the absent list, including any attendance notes or reasons provided.

Step 5: Export or share status

Export the present/absent summary for staffing support or share it with managers.

Tip: Use the present/absent report at the start of each shift to verify coverage and follow up on missing employees.
