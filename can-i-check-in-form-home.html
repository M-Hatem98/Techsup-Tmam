Yes, you can check in from home if your organization has designated home as an authorized work location and your schedule permits remote work.

How It Works

If your company supports remote work or home-based attendance:

  • Your administrator must add your home address as a valid work location in the system
  • Your home location will have a defined radius (typically 100-500 meters)
  • You can check in when your device GPS is within that radius

Requirements

  • GPS must be enabled on your mobile device
  • Your home location must be configured by your administrator
  • Your schedule must reflect home as a work location for that day

Important Notes

Home check-in is only available if:

  • Your company policy allows remote work
  • You have been approved to work from home
  • Your manager has authorized home as a work location for you

Tip: Contact your administrator if you need home added as a work location for authorized remote work days.
