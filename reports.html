The Reports section gives you quick access to attendance, leave, and exception analytics.

Step 1: Open the Reports menu

Go to Reports from the main navigation in the admin dashboard.

Step 2: Pick a report type

Select the report you need, such as attendance, leave, approvals, or exceptions.

Step 3: Apply filters

Set filters for date range, location, department, or employee groups.

Step 4: Review the results

Look at the report summary and use drilldown options to analyze the data.

Step 5: Export or schedule

Export the report to Excel, PDF, or schedule it for automated delivery.

Tip: Save frequently used filters as report templates for faster access.
