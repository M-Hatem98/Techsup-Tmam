Yes, Tamam supports role-based access control that allows HR users to view reports only for employees under their management.

HR User Roles

Tamam offers multiple HR access levels:

  • Global HR: Access to all employee data
  • Department HR: Access limited to their department
  • Location HR: Access limited to their location
  • Report-Only HR: Read-only access to specific reports

Configuring Restricted Access

  1. Go to User Management and create HR user accounts
  2. Assign HR role and access level
  3. Define departments or locations they manage
  4. Specify which reports they can access
  5. Set data visibility restrictions (reports, analytics, etc.)
  6. Save and activate the access configuration

Scope-Based Restrictions

HR users can be restricted to:

  • Specific departments (Finance, HR, IT, etc.)
  • Specific locations or branches
  • Specific report types
  • Specific time periods or date ranges
  • Export and download permissions

Benefits

  • Security through data compartmentalization
  • HR staff can focus on their responsibility area
  • Prevents unauthorized access to other departments
  • Maintains compliance with data protection policies
  • Audit trail of who accessed what data

Monitoring and Auditing

  • Track all data access by HR users
  • Generate access logs for compliance
  • Review permission usage regularly
  • Disable access when roles change

Tip: Regularly review HR user permissions to ensure they match current job responsibilities.
