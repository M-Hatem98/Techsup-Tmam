The work location attendance rate report shows attendance metrics by geographic location or workspace, helping you monitor where teams are performing best.

Step 1: Open Location Reports

Navigate to Reports and choose the work location attendance rate report to view attendance by location.

Step 2: Choose Location Groups

Select the location or group of locations you want to evaluate. This may include offices, warehouses, branches, or remote work sites.

Step 3: Set the Time Period

Pick a reporting period that fits your analysis, such as daily, weekly, or monthly. Consistent periods help compare trends accurately.

Step 4: Analyze Attendance Rates

Review key metrics such as present rate, absence rate, late check-ins, and early check-outs for each location.

Step 5: Use Insights for Planning

Use the location attendance rate data to make staffing, shift planning, and workplace decisions. Share findings with operations and HR teams.

Tip: Compare location rates over several weeks to spot persistent attendance patterns in specific teams or sites.
