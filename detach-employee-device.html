Detach Employee’s Device

Remove a device from an employee record when hardware is re-assigned or deactivated.

Detaching a device keeps your device inventory accurate and prevents the wrong employee from being linked to a device.

Step 1: Open employee profile

Find the employee in the directory and open their user profile page.

Step 2: Locate the device section

Scroll to the attached devices or biometric hardware section in the employee record.

Step 3: Select the device

Choose the device entry you want to detach from the employee.

Step 4: Detach or remove

Click Detach or Remove device and confirm the action.

Step 5: Verify the detachment

Confirm the device no longer appears in the employee profile and is available for reassignment.

Tip: Detach devices promptly when employees leave or change roles to prevent device conflicts.
