Access to employee information and attendance reports in Tamam is role-based and controlled by administrators based on organizational needs.

Standard Access Levels

Employees: Can view their own attendance records and reports

  • Personal attendance history
  • Check-in and check-out times
  • Leave balance and requests
  • Monthly attendance summaries

Line Managers: Can view their team's attendance

  • Direct reports' attendance records
  • Absence and late arrival notifications
  • Approval authority for leave requests
  • Team-level reports and analytics

HR / Administrators: Have full system access

  • All employee attendance data
  • Organization-wide reports
  • System configuration and settings
  • Audit logs and compliance reports

Privacy and Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access is logged and auditable
  • Unauthorized access is prevented and monitored
  • Regular security audits conducted

Customizing Access

Administrators can configure:

  • Who can view which reports
  • Department-level access restrictions
  • Custom role-based permissions
  • Data export restrictions

Tip: Contact your administrator if you need access to specific reports or data.
