Employee Tags

Learn how to use employee tags to categorize and filter people in Tamam.

Employee tags are a flexible way to group employees by attributes such as role, skill, location, or project. Tags make it easier to filter reports and manage employee data.

Step 1: Access Tags Settings

Open the employee management or settings area and choose Tags. This is where you can create and manage tag categories.

Step 2: Create Meaningful Tags

Define tags that reflect the way your organization works, such as remote, field, sales, or contractor. Keep tags consistent so filtering remains effective.

Step 3: Assign Tags to Employees

Add tags to employee profiles. You can assign multiple tags to a single employee if they belong to more than one group.

Step 4: Filter Reports with Tags

Use tags when filtering attendance, leave, or request reports. Tags help you narrow results to the employees who matter for your analysis.

Step 5: Update Tags as Needed

Review tags regularly and update them when roles or team structures change. Retire old tags that are no longer useful.

Tip: Standardize tag names in your organization to avoid duplicates like "Sales" and "sales" that make filtering harder.
