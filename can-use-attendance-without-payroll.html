Yes, organizations can choose the subscription that best fits their needs. Tamam allows customers to subscribe to the Attendance module only, or to subscribe to both Attendance and Payroll together.

Attendance Only Subscription

If your organization only needs attendance management, you can subscribe to the Attendance module without activating Payroll.

  • Employee check-in and check-out tracking.
  • Leave and attendance requests.
  • Shift and work schedule management.
  • Work location and attendance policy management.
  • Attendance reports and analytics.
  • Employee self-service attendance features.

Attendance + Payroll Subscription

Organizations that want a complete workforce management solution can subscribe to both Attendance and Payroll modules.

  • All Attendance module features.
  • Salary and compensation management.
  • Payroll processing and payroll records.
  • Payslip generation and distribution.
  • Employee loans and deductions management.
  • Payroll reports and export capabilities.

Choose What Fits Your Business

Whether you need only attendance tracking or a complete attendance and payroll solution, Tamam offers flexible subscription options that can grow with your organization's requirements.
