Yes, Tamam provides comprehensive reporting capabilities to generate detailed monthly reports on early/late check-ins and check-outs.

Report Types

Early/Late Check-In Report

  • Employees who arrived late
  • Number of late arrivals per employee
  • Minutes late for each incident
  • Trends and patterns over the month
  • Approval status of requests

Early Check-Out Report

  • Employees who left early
  • Minutes early for each checkout
  • Frequency of early departures
  • Manager approvals and permissions

How to Generate Reports

  1. Log in to Tamam admin portal
  2. Go to Reports section
  3. Select "Early/Late Report" or "Attendance Exceptions"
  4. Choose report period (specific month or date range)
  5. Select filters (employees, departments, locations)
  6. Choose output format (PDF, Excel, CSV)
  7. Generate and download report

Report Features

  • Filtering: By employee, department, location, or date
  • Sorting: By name, date, minutes late, frequency
  • Aggregation: Daily, weekly, or monthly summaries
  • Export: Excel, PDF, or CSV formats
  • Visualization: Charts and graphs for trends

Scheduling Reports

You can set up automatic reports:

  • Monthly reports sent automatically
  • Email distribution to managers and HR
  • Scheduled at specific times or dates
  • Recurring report generation

Report Analysis

Use these reports for:

  • Identifying chronic late arrivals
  • Performance management discussions
  • Compliance and policy enforcement
  • Scheduling optimization
  • Attendance improvement initiatives

Data Included

  • Employee name and ID
  • Date and time of check-in/check-out
  • Scheduled start/end time
  • Minutes late/early
  • Request or approval status
  • Attendance notes or comments

Tip: Schedule monthly reports to be automatically generated and sent to managers for easy monitoring.
