Attendance registration ensures your working hours and presence are recorded accurately.

Step 1: Open the attendance screen

From the app dashboard, navigate to the Attendance or Check-In section.

Step 2: Select the correct location

Choose the location or work site where you are registering attendance.

Step 3: Clock in or register

Tap the check-in or register button to record your start time.

Step 4: Check status

Confirm that the attendance status shows as present and your time is recorded.

Step 5: Clock out when finished

Use the same screen to clock out or register the end of your shift.

Tip: Register attendance from the approved location and avoid manual edits unless approved.
