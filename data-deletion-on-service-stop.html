When a client cancels their Tamam subscription, data handling is carefully managed according to our terms of service, data protection laws, and industry best practices.

Data Retention Period

Following service cancellation:

  • Data is retained for 30-90 days (as per contract terms)
  • Allows time for data backup and export
  • Provides grace period for service reinstatement
  • Enables compliance with regulatory requirements

Data Export Options

Before data deletion, you can:

  • Export all attendance records to CSV/Excel
  • Download historical reports and analytics
  • Export employee and organizational data
  • Request custom data formats
  • Receive data on external drive if needed

Data Deletion Process

  1. Service termination notice received
  2. Data export window opens (30-90 days)
  3. Export all needed data during this period
  4. Final deletion occurs after retention period
  5. Deletion confirmation provided

What Gets Deleted

  • Employee attendance records
  • Check-in/check-out logs
  • Leave and request history
  • User accounts and settings
  • System configuration and policies
  • All personally identifiable information (PII)

What Gets Retained

  • Anonymized usage statistics for service improvement
  • Billing and contract records (per legal requirements)
  • Audit logs required by law

Data Protection Compliance

  • Complies with GDPR and data protection laws
  • Secure deletion methods used
  • No unauthorized access during deletion
  • Encryption keys destroyed

Recommendations

  • Request data export immediately after cancellation
  • Download and store all historical reports
  • Plan data migration before termination date
  • Ensure compliance records are properly archived
  • Contact support for assistance with exports

Tip: If you're considering switching providers, plan your data export well in advance to ensure continuity.
