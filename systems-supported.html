Tamam is a cross-platform attendance management solution that works on multiple operating systems and devices.

Mobile Platforms

iOS

  • iPhone and iPad support
  • iOS 12.0 and later
  • Download from Apple App Store

Android

  • Android phones and tablets
  • Android 5.0 and later
  • Download from Google Play Store

Web Platforms

  • Windows computers and laptops
  • Mac (macOS)
  • Linux systems
  • Web browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge

Browser Requirements

For web access, ensure you have:

  • A modern web browser (updated within the last year)
  • JavaScript enabled
  • Internet connectivity
  • GPS enabled (for location-based features)

Compatibility

Tamam is compatible with:

  • HR management systems
  • Payroll software
  • ERP systems
  • Business intelligence tools

Tip: Keep your app or browser updated for the best performance and security features.
