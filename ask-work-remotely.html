Yes, Tamam supports remote work requests allowing employees to seek approval to work from locations other than the office.

Remote Work Request Process

  1. Open Tamam app or web portal
  2. Go to "Requests" or "Time Off" section
  3. Select "Remote Work" or "Work From Home"
  4. Choose the date(s) for remote work
  5. Add reason or justification if required
  6. Submit for manager approval
  7. Wait for approval notification

Remote Work Request Details

When submitting a remote work request, you can specify:

  • Date and duration of remote work
  • Work location (home, client site, etc.)
  • Contact details while working remotely
  • Justification or business reason
  • Any special arrangements needed

Manager Approval

After submission, your manager will:

  • Review the remote work request
  • Check against company policy and workload
  • Approve or deny the request
  • Send notification with decision
  • Add comments or conditions if needed

Approved Remote Work

Once approved:

  • Your schedule is marked as "Remote"
  • You check in from your remote location
  • Location-based attendance checks may be waived
  • You can work from anywhere during those hours
  • Attendance is tracked as normal

Hybrid Work Options

Organizations can configure:

  • Fully remote work days
  • Partial day remote arrangements
  • Flexible work hours with remote access
  • Location flexibility within work hours

Best Practices

  • Request remote work in advance when possible
  • Ensure reliable internet connection at home
  • Maintain communication with your team
  • Track your own hours and productivity
  • Follow company policies for remote work

Tip: Submit remote work requests early to allow time for manager consideration and approval.
