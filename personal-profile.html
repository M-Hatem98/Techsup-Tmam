Personal Profile

Update and review your personal information in Tamam.

Your personal profile holds key details that ensure your attendance and notifications are managed correctly.

Step 1: Open your profile

Go to the Profile or Account section from the app menu.

Step 2: Review your details

Check your name, email, phone number, and job-related information for accuracy.

Step 3: Update contact details

Make sure your contact info is current so notifications reach you.

Step 4: Save changes

Save any updates and wait for confirmation that your profile is updated.

Step 5: Check linked settings

Verify that your manager, department, and location assignments are correct.

Tip: Keep your profile updated to prevent delays in attendance approvals and important alerts.
