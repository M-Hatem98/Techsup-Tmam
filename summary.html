The summary section provides a snapshot of your latest attendance performance and any pending actions.

Step 1: Access the summary page

Open the Summary area from your employee dashboard.

Step 2: Review key metrics

Check your total hours, absences, late arrivals, and approved time off.

Step 3: Monitor pending approvals

See if there are any pending attendance corrections or manager approvals.

Step 4: Confirm your current status

Verify that your active schedule and location assignments are up to date.

Step 5: Follow next actions if needed

Use the summary to identify items that require follow-up or corrective requests.

Tip: Check your summary regularly to avoid surprises in your attendance reports.
