Need help?

Reach out for support if you need a guided walkthrough or custom onboarding plan.

Contact Support

Allows you to ask for corrections, time off, or schedule adjustments directly through the app. Follow our streamlined system to manage all your work requests efficiently.

01

Submit New Request

Initiate any dynamic request directly from your dashboard. Choose the appropriate category, set your target date range, and kickstart the approval workflow instantly.

02

Regularization Request

Forgot to clock in or had a technical issue with your attendance tracker? Use this feature to adjust your daily check-in/out logs and maintain an accurate timesheet.

03

Permission Request

Need to leave early, arrive late, or step out for a few hours? Submit a time-bound permission specifying the exact hours needed without burning through your annual balance.

04

Leave Request

Plan your extended time off, whether it’s casual, annual, or medical leave. The system automatically checks your available balance and forwards it to HR for final review.

05

Requests Summary

Track the lifecycles of all submitted tasks. Monitor real-time status badges (Pending, Approved, or Rejected), read manager feedback, and look over historical logs in one centralized hub.
