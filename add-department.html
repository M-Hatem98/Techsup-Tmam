Departments help structure your organization and make attendance reporting easier to manage.

Step 1: Open Departments

From the admin dashboard, navigate to the Departments section under organization settings.

Step 2: Create a new department

Click Add Department, then enter the department name and an optional description.

Step 3: Assign a manager

Choose a manager or team lead for the department if your workflow requires approval and reporting ownership.

Step 4: Add employees

Assign employees to the department either during creation or by editing employee profiles after saving.

Step 5: Save and verify

Save the department and confirm it appears in the department list and employee group views.

Tip: Use departments to improve report filtering and to manage permissions consistently across teams.
