HR Live Dashboard Statistics

Monitor employee attendance, commitment rates, employee happiness, and daily HR activity in real time from a single dashboard.

The HR Live Dashboard Statistics page gives administrators a complete overview of workforce activity, attendance trends, and employee engagement metrics.

Step 1: Access the Dashboard

Open the HR dashboard from the admin sidebar menu to view live company statistics and workforce analytics.

Step 2: Review Attendance Status

The Attendance Status section displays the total number of employees currently tracked in the system along with overall attendance progress indicators.

Step 3: Monitor Daily Employee Activity

The dashboard cards provide quick summaries of:

  • Check-In: Displays employees currently checked in.
  • Holiday: Shows workers currently on holiday.
  • Absent: Displays employees marked absent.

Step 4: Analyze Attendance Rate

The Attendance Rate chart visualizes employee attendance activity throughout the week. Administrators can compare:

  • Employee check-ins
  • Holiday attendance records
  • Absent employees

This chart helps identify attendance patterns and workforce behavior across multiple days.

Step 5: Track Commitment Rate

The Commitment Rate panel displays the overall employee commitment percentage for the current week using a circular progress chart.

This metric helps management evaluate workforce consistency and operational discipline.

Step 6: Review Employee Feelings

The Employee Feelings section measures employee satisfaction and happiness levels inside the company using a visual gauge indicator.

HR teams can use this information to improve workplace culture and employee engagement.
