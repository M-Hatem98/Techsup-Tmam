This guide helps you inspect attendance evidence linked to a single employee.

Step 1: Search the employee

Locate the employee record in the attendance or proof evidence section.

Step 2: Choose the date range

Select the relevant time period for the employee proof review.

Step 3: Review proof entries

Inspect each submission and confirm the attendance data matches the employee's record.

Step 4: Validate evidence

Confirm whether the proof meets your policy and mark it accordingly.

Step 5: Add notes

Record the final status and any comments for the employee file or audit trail.

Tip: Use individual proof checks when attendance issues are disputed or require follow-up.
