Notifications keep you informed about attendance approvals, requests, and status updates.

Step 1: Check your notification center

Open the Notifications area in the app.

Step 2: Review new alerts

Read messages about request approvals, schedule updates, and attendance reminders.

Step 3: Manage notification settings

Adjust your notification preferences so you receive alerts by email, SMS, or app.

Step 4: Mark items read

Mark completed alerts as read to keep your notification list organized.

Step 5: Follow action items

Take any required next steps from the notifications, such as approving requests or updating details.

Tip: Keep notifications enabled to stay up to date with your attendance status and approvals.
