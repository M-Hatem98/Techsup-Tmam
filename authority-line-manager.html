This guide explains what a line manager can do in Tamam and how employees should work with them.

Step 1: Identify your line manager

Check the employee profile section to see who is listed as your line manager.

Step 2: Understand approvals

Line managers can approve leave, attendance adjustments, and request changes on behalf of their team.

Step 3: Use comments and notes

Include a note for your line manager when submitting requests so they understand the context.

Step 4: Monitor request status

Track request progress in the Requests section and see when your line manager has reviewed it.

Step 5: Communicate changes

Speak with your line manager if you receive feedback or need to update request details.

Tip: Clear communication with your line manager speeds up approvals and keeps attendance records accurate.
