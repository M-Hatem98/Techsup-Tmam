If you forget your mobile device, you have several options to handle your attendance record.

Options Available

1. Web Portal Check-In

If your organization has enabled web-based check-in, you can:

  • Access Tamam through a web browser on any computer
  • Check in from the office or any authorized location
  • This option may require location verification

2. Manual Check-In via Admin

Your manager or administrator can:

  • Manually check you in through the admin portal
  • This is typically logged and auditable
  • Requires appropriate permissions and authorization

3. Submit a Correction Request

You can:

  • Submit an attendance correction request after the fact
  • Provide details about your absence or late check-in
  • Wait for manager approval

Best Practice

To avoid attendance issues:

  • Always carry your mobile device with you
  • Keep your device charged throughout the day
  • Notify your manager immediately if you cannot check in

Tip: Contact your administrator about alternative check-in methods available in your organization.
