Yes, employees can view and access their personal attendance reports in Tamam, providing transparency and accountability for their records.

What Employees Can View

  • Daily check-in and check-out times
  • Total hours worked each day
  • Monthly attendance summaries
  • Leave balance and history
  • Late arrivals and absences
  • Pending approvals and requests
  • Year-to-date attendance statistics

Accessing Reports

  1. Log into Tamam mobile app or web portal
  2. Go to "My Reports" or "Attendance" section
  3. Select date range for the report
  4. View attendance data in various formats
  5. Export or print reports as needed

Report Types Available

Daily Report: Check-in/out times for each day

Monthly Report: Summary of attendance for the month

Leave Report: All approved and pending leave

Statistics: Attendance trends and patterns

Benefits

  • Transparency in attendance records
  • Self-service access to your data
  • Ability to dispute errors quickly
  • Documentation for personal records
  • Easy export for external use

Privacy

  • Employees can only see their own data
  • Managers see only their reports
  • Reports are encrypted and secure

Tip: Regularly review your attendance reports to ensure accuracy and catch any discrepancies.
