Filtering Before Exporting the Early/Late Check-in and Check-out Report

Use the right filters before export to get accurate early/late attendance data.

Applying filters before exporting the early/late report helps administrators focus on the most relevant attendance exceptions and save time when analyzing results.

Step 1: Open the Early/Late Report

Navigate to Reports and select the early/late check-in and check-out report to load the attendance exception details.

Step 2: Select the Time Range

Choose the correct date range for the period you want to export, such as a week or month. This ensures the export contains only the needed data.

Step 3: Filter by Employee and Location

Use filters for departments, individual employees, work locations, or specific teams to narrow the dataset and avoid overwhelming results.

Step 4: Refine with Attendance Status

Filter by early check-ins, late check-ins, early check-outs, or late check-outs depending on the issue you want to analyze.

Step 5: Preview Then Export

Review the filtered data on screen before exporting. Confirm the exported file format and field selection for the most useful report output.

Tip: Previewing filtered results before export saves time and prevents large, hard-to-read files from being generated unnecessarily.
