Add Employee

Add new staff to Tamam with the right role, department, and attendance access.

Adding employees correctly ensures attendance tracking, permissions, and onboarding work smoothly.

Step 1: Open Employees

From the admin dashboard, go to the Employees area to manage user records.

Step 2: Start a new employee record

Click Add Employee and fill in name, email, ID number, and contact details.

Step 3: Assign department and location

Choose the employee's department, location, and reporting manager during setup.

Step 4: Set roles and permissions

Choose the user role and access level so the employee can use the appropriate Tamam features.

Step 5: Save and invite

Save the employee record and optionally send an invitation so the employee can complete onboarding.

Tip: Verify contact details and role settings before saving to avoid permission issues later.
