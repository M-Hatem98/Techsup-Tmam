Yes, line managers can view their team members' attendance records in Tamam, enabling them to monitor performance and approve leave requests.

What Managers Can View

Managers typically have access to:

  • Daily check-in and check-out times for their team
  • Late arrivals and early departures
  • Absences and missing check-ins
  • Approved and pending leave requests
  • Attendance summary and reports for their department
  • Performance trends and exceptions

Manager Responsibilities

Using the attendance data, managers can:

  • Review employee attendance patterns
  • Identify and address chronic late arrivals
  • Approve or deny leave requests
  • Approve attendance corrections and exceptions
  • Generate team performance reports
  • Investigate attendance issues

Permissions

Manager access is based on:

  • Their direct report relationships in the system
  • Departmental hierarchy assignments
  • Role-based permissions granted by administrators

Tip: Managers should review team attendance regularly to catch issues early and support their team.
