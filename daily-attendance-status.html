Daily Attendance Status

Check employee attendance for any specific day in one quick view.

Daily Attendance Status allows administrators to quickly view who was present, absent, or on leave for a given date, helping with operational decision-making.

Step 1: Open Daily Attendance Dashboard

Navigate to the attendance section and select Daily Status to view today's or any specific day's attendance snapshot.

Step 2: Choose the Date

Select the date you want to review. You can check today, yesterday, or any date in the past for historical reference.

Step 3: View Summary Metrics

Check the totals for present employees, absent employees, on-leave employees, and those with pending status.

Step 4: Identify Missing Check-Ins

Look for employees who haven't checked in yet. Follow up with them or their managers if the day is nearly over.

Step 5: Filter by Department or Location

Use filters to focus on specific teams or sites. This helps address attendance issues at the local level.

Tip: Check daily status each morning to identify coverage issues early and plan accordingly for the day.
