This guide explains how users can securely log in to the system and reset their password if they forget their account credentials.

Step 1: Open the Login Page

Navigate to the system login page using the provided company URL or portal link.

Step 2: Enter Login Credentials

Type your registered email address or username along with your password in the login form fields.

Step 3: Access Your Dashboard

Click the login button to access your dashboard and available system modules based on your account permissions.

Step 4: Reset Forgotten Password

If you forget your password, click on the Forgot Password option available on the login page.

Step 5: Verify Your Account

Enter your registered email address to receive a password reset link or verification instructions.

Step 6: Create a New Password

Open the reset link sent to your email and create a new secure password that meets the system security requirements.

Step 7: Login with the New Password

Return to the login page and sign in again using your updated password credentials.

Tip: Use a strong password containing uppercase letters, numbers, and special characters to improve account security.
