Application Login

Learn how to log into the Tamam application safely and consistently.

Login is your first step to accessing attendance, requests, and personal profile features.

Step 1: Open the Tamam app

Launch the Tamam web or mobile application from the login page.

Step 2: Enter credentials

Use your assigned email or username along with your secure password.

Step 3: Use two-factor authentication

If your organization requires it, complete the additional authentication step.

Step 4: Recover a password

If you forget your credentials, use the Forgot Password link to reset them.

Step 5: Confirm successful login

Once logged in, verify your name and profile appear in the app header.

Tip: Bookmark the Tamam login page for faster access and avoid using shared passwords.
